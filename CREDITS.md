## 3D models

Everything in `assets/kenney/` is by [Kenney](https://kenney.nl) and released
under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (no credit
required, credited anyway). Each folder has the pack's `License.txt`. The Tower Defense Kit's
`colormap.png` has its grass greens shifted from teal to a warmer green, and the Graveyard Kit's lamp post uses a grey copy of
its palette (`colormap_lamp.png`).
